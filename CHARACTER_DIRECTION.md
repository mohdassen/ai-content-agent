# Official Presenter — خلف الرقم

## Approved visual identity
The official presenter is the second approved concept: a polished semi-realistic 3D/2.5D male tech presenter with dark styled hair, short groomed beard, rectangular dark glasses, navy/dark blazer over a black crew-neck shirt, dark trousers, clean white sneakers, and a subtle smartwatch.

## Personality
- Intelligent, approachable, confident — never childish or exaggerated.
- Modern Saudi/Arabic technology-channel tone without imitating a real person.
- Explains complex numbers simply and visually.
- Natural facial expressions and restrained hand gestures.

## On-screen language
The presenter must not stay centered for the whole reel. Use him as a storytelling device:
1. Hook: medium shot, direct eye contact, one clear gesture.
2. Explanation: move presenter to left/right third while charts, numbers and documentary visuals occupy the other side.
3. Evidence/B-roll moments: presenter may disappear completely.
4. Key number: presenter points or looks toward the animated number/chart.
5. Closing: presenter returns briefly with خلف الرقم branding.

## Visual environment
Dark premium technology studio / data-center environment. Navy, cyan and restrained gold highlights. Avoid flat green backgrounds and generic PowerPoint-style panels.

## Required pose/expression set
- neutral explanation
- open-hand explanation
- point left
- point right
- thoughtful
- surprised/key insight
- welcoming/closing

## Animation target
Use subtle breathing/body drift, blinking, head movement and hand gesture changes. Lip-sync is a later enhancement; narration timing remains the source of truth. Character motion must never delay or break the audio pipeline.

## Consistency rule
Future character assets must preserve the same face, hairstyle, glasses, beard, blazer/shirt combination and overall proportions. Do not regenerate a visibly different presenter from scene to scene.
